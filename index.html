Our Story

About Utopia Wellness & Gifts

For nearly 30 years, Utopia has been part of North Vancouver's story — a place where people come to find meaningful gifts, moments of clarity, and a sense of connection.

The shop was first opened in the 1990s by Donna King near Lonsdale Key Market. Over the years it moved along Lonsdale Avenue, first to 3rd Street and later to its current home at 1826 Lonsdale Avenue. In 2021, Wendy took over the store when it was known as Utopia Mystical Sanctuary and renamed it Utopia Sacred Space.

Today, under the care of Vinod and Olga, the store has been thoughtfully renovated and reintroduced as Utopia Wellness & Gifts. While the name and focus have evolved, the heart of the shop remains the same: offering carefully chosen products, a welcoming space, and genuine support for the community.

We carry premium gifts, crystals, protection and prosperity items, tarot cards, and wellness tools, and we also host Clarity & Guidance sessions and community circles. Whether you visit us in person or shop online, you'll experience the difference of an independent store with a long local history.

Thank you for helping us continue this North Vancouver legacy.
