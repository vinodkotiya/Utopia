# Blog Rewrite Task Checklist

Read this file BEFORE writing each blog post. Follow every step in order.

---

## STEP 1: READ THE SOURCE

- Open `d:\temp\blog final\output\source.md`
- Find the entry by serial number (search for `=== #71 ===` etc.)
- Read the CONTENT section for that entry
- If it says CREATE FROM TOPIC, derive the article from the TARGET filename and topic
- Note the key points, structure, and subtopics covered in the source content

## STEP 2: PLAN THE ARTICLE

- Decide on a new H1 (primary keyword, natural, under 60 chars if possible)
- Decide on 3-5 H2 sections based on what the source covers (do NOT use generic headings)
- Plan bullet lists where the source had lists or multiple points
- Plan the FAQ based on actual questions someone searching this topic would ask

## STEP 3: WRITE THE ARTICLE (Rules)

- First 2 sentences MUST answer the core question directly
- NO em dashes (use commas, periods, semicolons, or "and" instead)
- NO sentences that sound like AI (avoid: "In this article", "Let's dive in", "Whether you're a seasoned...", "In today's world", "It's important to note")
- Write as if Utopia wrote this from scratch on day one
- 800-1800 words of article body content
- Short paragraphs (2-4 sentences max)
- Bold key terms naturally
- Each H2 must have 2-4 paragraphs of real substance OR a meaningful list

## STEP 4: REQUIRED STRUCTURE

```
- Breadcrumb (Home > Blog > [Short Title])
- H1
- Byline: By Utopia Wellness & Gifts · [Date]
- Paragraph 1 (answer the question)
- Paragraph 2 (context and why it matters)
- INLINE CTA box
- H2 section (real content, multiple paragraphs or list)
- H2 section (real content)
- H2 section (real content)
- [Optional: more H2s as needed]
- H2: Frequently Asked Questions
  - 3-4 unique H3 questions with concise answers
- BOTTOM CTA box
- Prev/Next nav
```

## STEP 5: CONTENT RULES

- Remove ALL mentions of: Purple Garden, purplegarden, other psychic platforms, other company names, original authors, guest writers, third-party sites
- Remove ALL external links (keep only Utopia internal links)
- Remove: Summary blocks, Key Takeaways, "In this article" blocks, Table of Contents
- Do NOT copy sentences from source. Fully rewrite
- Author always: Utopia Wellness & Gifts
- No medical claims, no guaranteed predictions, no fear-based language
- Mention Utopia naturally 2-3 times in the body (not forced)

## WHAT "DONE" MEANS

Each output HTML is:
- Complete (not a stub or template)
- Unique (content not repeated from other articles)
- On-brand (warm, grounded, practical Utopia voice)
- Clean (no Purple Garden, no external links, no summaries)
- Substantive (800+ words of real article content with topic-specific H2s)
- Publishable (could go live today without edits)
