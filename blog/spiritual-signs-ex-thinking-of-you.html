7 Spiritual Signs Your Ex Is Still Thinking About You

By Utopia Wellness & Gifts · June 12, 2026

Sometimes after a relationship ends, you can't shake the feeling that your ex is still thinking about you. Maybe they cross your mind at the strangest moments — not because you're dwelling on them, but because something energetic seems to pull your attention their way. From a spiritual perspective, these experiences aren't random. When someone holds you in their thoughts with intensity, that focused energy can ripple across the invisible threads that once connected you.

This isn't about obsessing over an ex or hoping they come back. It's about awareness — understanding what you're experiencing so you can decide how to respond from a place of clarity rather than confusion. Below are seven spiritual signs that someone from your past may still be directing their energy toward you, along with guidance on what to do with that awareness.

1. Random, Intrusive Thoughts of Them

You're going about your day — maybe grocery shopping, working, or reading — and suddenly your ex pops into your mind with no apparent trigger. This isn't the same as missing them during quiet moments. It feels more like a tap on the shoulder from an unseen presence. The thought arrives fully formed, almost as if it was placed there rather than generated by your own mind.

Energetically, this can happen when someone is thinking about you with strong emotion — whether love, regret, or longing. Their focused thought creates a kind of energetic ping that you receive intuitively. Pay attention to when these moments happen; they may correspond with times your ex is particularly focused on memories of your relationship.

2. Vivid Dreams Featuring Your Ex

Dreams are one of the most common channels through which spiritual connections express themselves. If your ex appears in your dreams with unusual clarity — conversations that feel real, emotional exchanges that linger after waking — this may indicate an active energetic link. Unlike regular processing dreams (where your subconscious sorts through memories), these feel vivid, present-tense, and emotionally charged.

Some spiritual practitioners believe that when two people share a strong soul connection, they can meet in the dream space — especially when one person is intensely thinking about the other before sleep. If you wake from these dreams feeling like you actually interacted with your ex, it's worth noting in a journal.

3. Feeling Their Energy or Presence

Have you ever felt a sudden warmth, a tingling sensation, or a general feeling of being "watched" — not in a threatening way, but in a familiar one? This can be your ex's energy reaching you across distance. When someone holds you in their awareness with genuine emotion, sensitive people often feel it physically — as a tightening in the chest, warmth on the skin, or a distinct sense of their presence nearby.

This is particularly common with people you shared deep intimacy with, as the energetic cords between you remain intact until intentionally released. If these sensations come at specific times — late evening, early morning — they may align with moments your ex is thinking of you most intensely.

4. Seeing Their Name Everywhere

Suddenly their name appears on a coffee cup, a licence plate, a song credit, or a random social media post. While confirmation bias can play a role here, the spiritual interpretation holds that when someone directs strong thought-energy toward you, the universe mirrors that connection through synchronicity. Their name becomes a symbol that your awareness is drawn to as a gentle nudge.

The key distinction is frequency and timing. If you're seeing their name (or hearing it) multiple times in a short period — especially during moments when you're otherwise not thinking about them — it may be more than coincidence. Trust your intuition about whether these feel significant.

5. Unexpected Emotional Waves

You're feeling fine — and then a wave of sadness, nostalgia, or even love washes over you without any apparent cause. These emotional surges can feel foreign, as though they don't entirely belong to you. From an energetic standpoint, empathic and intuitive people can pick up on the emotions of those who are thinking about them — especially if the emotional bond was once very deep.

If you suddenly feel tearful, nostalgic, or strangely tender toward your ex when you had been feeling neutral, it's worth pausing to notice. Ask yourself: "Is this mine?" Learning to distinguish your own emotions from received energy is a valuable spiritual skill — and one our readers at Utopia can help you develop during a clarity session.

6. Synchronicities and Meaningful Coincidences

Synchronicities go beyond just seeing a name. They include things like: your ex's favourite song playing at a significant moment, running into their friends unexpectedly, stumbling across an old photo you didn't know existed, or encountering repeated references to places you shared together. These meaningful coincidences can signal that the energetic connection between you is still active.

  • Repeated number patterns — Seeing 11:11 or other angel numbers at moments you think of them
  • Mutual friends reaching out — People from that chapter of life suddenly reappearing
  • Shared symbols — Finding objects or seeing images that held meaning only for you two
  • Timeline echoes — Important dates or anniversaries triggering unusual events

7. "Your Song" Keeps Finding You

Music carries powerful emotional energy. If a song that was meaningful to your relationship keeps playing — in shops, on the radio, in a random playlist — it can feel like the universe is delivering a message. Sound vibrates at frequencies that transcend physical distance, and songs act as energetic anchors to specific people, memories, and emotions.

When your ex thinks of you while hearing "your song," that emotional charge can create a ripple that draws the same song into your awareness. It's one of the most commonly reported signs among people who feel they share a soul-level bond with a former partner.

What to Do When You Notice These Signs

Recognizing these signs is about self-awareness, not obsession. The goal isn't to fixate on your ex or use these signs as reasons to reach out. Instead, use them as information to help you understand your own energetic landscape. Here's a healthy approach:

  • Acknowledge without attaching — Notice the sign, honour it, then let it pass without building a story around it
  • Protect your energy — If the connection feels draining, consider cord-cutting or energy clearing work
  • Seek clarity — A tarot reading or clarity session can help you understand what these signs mean for your specific path
  • Focus on your own healing — Redirect your energy toward personal growth rather than waiting for external validation

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you really feel when someone is thinking about you?

Many spiritual traditions and energy workers believe that yes, you can sense when someone directs strong thought-energy toward you. This is especially common between people who shared deep emotional or intimate bonds. The experience varies — some people feel physical sensations, while others receive intuitive nudges, dreams, or synchronicities.

How do I know if it's a real sign or just wishful thinking?

The honest answer: it can be difficult to tell on your own. Genuine signs tend to arrive unexpectedly when you're not actively thinking about your ex. They feel surprising rather than sought-after. If you find yourself constantly looking for signs, that's more likely your mind searching for confirmation. A clarity session with an experienced reader can help you distinguish between genuine energetic signals and emotional projection.

Should I contact my ex if I notice these signs?

Not necessarily. These signs are information about energy, not instructions to act. Before reaching out, consider whether contact would genuinely serve your wellbeing and growth. A clarity session can help you explore the situation from a grounded perspective and decide what action (if any) aligns with your highest good.

How can I stop feeling my ex's energy if I want to move on?

Energetic cord-cutting, intention-setting rituals, and energy clearing practices can help you release lingering connections. Working with protective crystals like black tourmaline or obsidian can also create energetic boundaries. At Utopia, we offer both guidance sessions and tools to support you in releasing old connections and reclaiming your energy fully.
