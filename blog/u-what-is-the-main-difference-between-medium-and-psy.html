Medium vs Psychic: Understanding the Key Differences

May 31, 2027

The core difference is this: all mediums are psychic, but not all psychics are mediums. A psychic perceives information about living people, current situations, and probable futures through extrasensory perception. A medium does everything a psychic does but additionally communicates with the spirits of those who have died. The medium's distinguishing ability is bridging the gap between the physical world and the spirit world, delivering messages from the deceased to the living.

Understanding this distinction helps you choose the right practitioner for your needs. If you want guidance about your life situation, career, or relationships, a psychic reading serves you well. If you specifically want to connect with someone who has passed, you need a medium.

What a Psychic Does

A psychic perceives information beyond the five ordinary senses. Their abilities may include:

Clairvoyance: Seeing images, symbols, or scenes related to your situation.

Clairsentience: Feeling emotions or physical sensations that convey information about you.

Clairaudience: Hearing words, names, or messages relevant to your question.

Claircognizance: Knowing information without a sensory source, receiving complete understanding instantly.

Psychics read your energy field, perceive patterns in your current situation, and offer guidance based on probable trajectories. They work with the living: your energy, your situation, your probable future. They do not typically communicate with the deceased unless they also possess mediumistic ability.

What a Medium Does

A medium possesses psychic ability plus the specific capacity to perceive and communicate with discarnate spirits (people who have died). During a mediumship session, the medium:

Connects with specific spirits. They do not choose who comes through. The spirits who are present and willing to communicate determine who appears. However, setting intention for a specific connection increases likelihood.

Provides evidence of identity. A genuine medium delivers information that confirms the spirit's identity: physical descriptions, personality traits, shared memories, names, or specific details that only the deceased and the sitter would know.

Delivers messages. Beyond proving identity, the medium relays what the spirit wants to communicate: expressions of love, guidance, apologies, reassurances about their wellbeing, or information the living person needs.

Describes the spirit's current state. Many mediums perceive how the spirit feels now (at peace, apologetic, watching over family) and can convey their ongoing relationship with the living.

How to Choose Between a Psychic and a Medium

Choose a psychic when: You want guidance about your life direction, career decisions, relationship dynamics, or personal growth. You are not seeking contact with anyone deceased. You want insight into your own situation and probable outcomes.

Choose a medium when: You want to connect with a specific person who has passed. You are grieving and seeking confirmation that your loved one continues to exist. You need closure, a message, or the peace that comes from knowing they are okay.

Choose either when: You want general spiritual guidance and are open to whatever form it takes. Many practitioners offer both psychic reading and mediumship within a single session depending on what comes through.

Red Flags for Both

  • Claiming 100% accuracy (no practitioner is always correct)
  • Cold reading techniques (fishing for information through vague questions)
  • Pressure to purchase additional services
  • Fear-based statements designed to create urgency
  • Inability to provide specific, verifiable information

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a psychic connect with my deceased loved one if they are not specifically a medium?

Generally no. Mediumship requires a specific ability beyond general psychic perception. Some psychics may receive brief impressions from spirits, but sustained, evidential spirit communication requires developed mediumistic capacity.

How do I know if a medium is genuine?

Genuine mediums provide specific, verifiable evidence of the spirit's identity without fishing for information. They describe appearances, personalities, and shared memories that they could not know through ordinary means. Specificity and accuracy (rather than vague generalities) indicate genuine connection.

Can mediums communicate with anyone who has passed?

Mediums can typically connect with any spirit willing to communicate, but not every spirit comes through in every session. Timing, the spirit's willingness, and the energetic conditions all influence who presents. Specific connections cannot be guaranteed.
