The Wheel of Fortune Tarot Card: Cycles, Luck, and Turning Points

May 21, 2027

The Wheel of Fortune is Major Arcana X, and it represents the cyclical nature of life, fate, turning points, luck (both good and challenging), and the recognition that everything changes. When this card appears, it signals that a significant shift is occurring in your circumstances. The wheel turns regardless of your actions, bringing a new phase that may feel like luck or like fate, but either way arrives with a sense of inevitability.

This card teaches that nothing is permanent. The highs do not last forever, but neither do the lows. Whatever your current situation, the wheel is turning. If life has been difficult, improvement is approaching. If life has been comfortable, change is still coming. The Wheel of Fortune does not judge the change as good or bad. It simply confirms that movement is the fundamental nature of existence.

Symbolism in The Wheel of Fortune

The wheel itself represents the endless cycle of change. Seasons turn. Fortunes rise and fall. Situations that seem permanent reveal themselves as temporary. The wheel never stops moving.

The four figures in the corners (lion, eagle, bull, angel) represent the fixed signs of the zodiac and the four elements, suggesting that this cycle operates across all dimensions of life simultaneously. No life area is exempt from the wheel's turning.

The sphinx atop the wheel represents wisdom, the ability to remain centred and discerning regardless of where the wheel positions you. True wisdom knows that both fortune and misfortune are temporary states within a larger cycle.

The serpent descending represents the descent into material challenges or karmic lessons. The figure ascending represents rising fortune. Both movements are natural and both serve growth.

The letters TARO (or ROTA) on the wheel suggest the connection between tarot wisdom and understanding life's rotations. The tarot itself is a wheel of wisdom offering perspective on cyclical change.

The Wheel of Fortune Upright: Change Arriving

Upright, the Wheel brings:

A turning point. Something in your life is shifting significantly. This is not gradual evolution but a distinct moment where circumstances change direction. Recognise it as such rather than treating it as minor fluctuation.

Good fortune or improved luck. Often the Wheel upright indicates that fortune is turning in your favour. Opportunities arrive. Circumstances improve. What was stuck begins to move. This luck is not random; it is the wheel completing its rotation back toward the light.

Karma and consequences. What you have put into motion (through past actions, intentions, and choices) is now returning to you. If you have acted with integrity, the returns are positive. The Wheel delivers what has been earned.

Fate and destiny. Some events cannot be prevented or manufactured. They simply arrive because it is time. The Wheel represents these destined moments where life moves regardless of your plans.

In love: A significant shift in romantic circumstances. A new relationship phase beginning. Meeting someone at a fated moment. The relationship reaching a turning point.

In career: Professional circumstances changing. Promotion, opportunity, or a shift in career direction arriving. The career wheel turning toward a new chapter.

The Wheel of Fortune Reversed: Resistance to Change

Reversed, the Wheel indicates:

Bad luck or downward turn. The wheel's rotation brings challenge rather than ease. Circumstances shift unfavourably. This is temporary (the wheel keeps turning) but uncomfortable during its passage.

Resistance to inevitable change. You are fighting against a shift that cannot be prevented. The resistance creates suffering without altering the outcome. Acceptance reduces pain even when it cannot change circumstances.

Feeling stuck or stagnant. The wheel has stopped turning, or feels as though it has. Movement that should be happening is blocked or delayed. Patience and willingness to release control often restart the wheel.

Karma returning uncomfortably. Past actions producing consequences you would rather avoid. The Wheel reversed asks you to accept responsibility and move through the discomfort toward resolution.

Working With Wheel of Fortune Energy

The Wheel teaches non-attachment. When fortune rises, enjoy it without clinging. When fortune falls, endure it without despair. Both states are temporary. The wisdom position is the sphinx at the top: observing the rotation with equanimity rather than being thrown about by each turn.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Wheel of Fortune about literal luck?

Partially. It encompasses luck, fate, karma, and cyclical change. The "luck" it brings often reflects what you have previously invested returning to you rather than random fortune from nowhere. What appears as luck frequently has roots in past action.

Can I influence where the wheel takes me?

You can influence through consistent right action (building good karma), through intention-setting during turning points, and through your response to whatever arrives. You cannot stop the wheel from turning, but you can choose how you ride it.

Does the Wheel of Fortune predict timing?

It indicates that change is imminent rather than distant. The turning point is close or already occurring. The Wheel does not specify dates but confirms that the shift belongs to the immediate future rather than months away.
