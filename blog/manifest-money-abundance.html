How to Manifest Money and Abundance Into Your Life

By Utopia Wellness & Gifts · May 5, 2026

Manifesting money starts with aligning your mindset, energy, and daily actions with a sense of abundance rather than scarcity. It isn't about wishful thinking — it's about creating intentional shifts in how you relate to wealth, worthiness, and receiving.

At Utopia Wellness & Gifts in North Vancouver, we see people transform their relationship with money through a blend of practical tools — crystals, intention-setting rituals, and clarity sessions. Whether you're clearing old limiting beliefs or building a new prosperity practice, the journey begins with awareness.

What Does It Mean to Manifest Money?

Manifestation is the practice of directing your thoughts, emotions, and energy toward a desired outcome. When it comes to money, that means releasing feelings of lack and actively choosing beliefs that support financial flow. It's not magic — it's consistent inner work paired with practical steps.

Many spiritual traditions teach that our external world reflects our internal state. If you carry deep-seated beliefs like "money is hard to come by" or "I don't deserve wealth," those patterns tend to show up in your experiences. Manifestation work invites you to examine and rewire those patterns.

Practical Ways to Attract Abundance

  • Set a clear financial intention — Write down a specific amount or goal. Clarity gives energy a direction.
  • Use abundance affirmations daily — Phrases like "I am open to receiving" or "Money flows to me easily" retrain your subconscious.
  • Create an abundance altar — Place citrine, green aventurine, or pyrite alongside a written intention on a dedicated surface in your home.
  • Practice gratitude for what you have — Appreciating current resources opens the door for more to arrive.
  • Release fear around money — Journaling, energy healing, or a clarity session can help you identify and dissolve financial blocks.
  • Take aligned action — Manifestation works best when paired with real-world steps: updating your resume, launching that project, or investing in yourself.

Best Crystals for Money Manifestation

Crystals can serve as physical reminders of your intention and are believed to hold specific energetic properties. Here are a few favourites we recommend at Utopia:

  • Citrine — Often called the "merchant's stone," citrine is associated with prosperity, confidence, and solar plexus energy.
  • Pyrite — Known for its golden shimmer, pyrite symbolises wealth and willpower. Keep a piece on your desk or in your wallet.
  • Green Aventurine — A heart-chakra stone linked to opportunity and luck, especially in new ventures.
  • Tiger's Eye — Supports focus, courage, and grounded decision-making around finances.

Visit our crystal collection online or browse in-store on Lonsdale Ave to find the stone that resonates with your abundance goals.

How a Clarity Session Can Help You Manifest

Sometimes the biggest block to abundance is one you can't see yourself. A Clarity & Guidance Session with one of our experienced readers can help you uncover hidden patterns — inherited money beliefs, energetic blocks, or unconscious habits — that keep you stuck in a scarcity loop.

Our readers use tarot, intuition, and energy work to offer perspective and practical next steps. Many clients leave with a renewed sense of clarity around career decisions, investments, or simply feeling worthy of financial ease.

Building a Daily Abundance Ritual

Consistency matters more than intensity. A simple daily practice can shift your energetic baseline over time:

  • Spend two minutes each morning holding your abundance crystal and visualising your financial goal as already achieved.
  • Write three things you're grateful for that relate to money or resources.
  • Repeat one abundance affirmation before you start your workday.
  • At the end of the week, note any unexpected income, gifts, or opportunities — however small.

Over time, this practice trains your brain to notice abundance rather than lack, which naturally influences the choices you make and the opportunities you attract.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to manifest money?

There's no fixed timeline. Some people notice shifts within days, while for others it takes weeks of consistent practice. The key factors are clarity of intention, emotional alignment, and willingness to take action when opportunities appear.

Which crystal is best for attracting money?

Citrine is the most popular crystal for money manifestation due to its association with the solar plexus chakra, confidence, and prosperity energy. Pyrite and green aventurine are also excellent choices depending on whether you need willpower or new opportunities.

Can a tarot reading help with financial decisions?

Yes. A tarot reading can illuminate your current energy around finances, reveal hidden blocks, and highlight areas where you might take action. It's not about predicting lottery numbers — it's about gaining perspective on your relationship with money.

Do I need to believe in manifestation for it to work?

An open mind helps. At minimum, the practical elements — goal clarity, gratitude, and aligned action — benefit everyone regardless of spiritual belief. The energetic and spiritual layers add depth for those who resonate with that approach.
