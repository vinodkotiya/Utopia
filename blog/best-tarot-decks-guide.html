The Best Tarot Decks for Every Reader: Our Top Picks

Written by Utopia Wellness & Gifts · August 28, 2026

Choosing a tarot deck is a deeply personal decision. The best tarot deck for you is one that speaks to your intuition, resonates with your aesthetic sensibility, and feels comfortable in your hands. Whether you are pulling your first card or you have been reading for decades, the right deck can transform your practice.

With hundreds of tarot decks available today, narrowing down your options can feel overwhelming. We have curated our top recommendations based on years of working with readers at all levels here at Utopia Wellness & Gifts in North Vancouver. From timeless classics to stunning modern interpretations, these are the decks we recommend most often and why.

Timeless Classic Decks Every Reader Should Know

These decks have stood the test of time and form the foundation of tarot reading tradition. They are excellent starting points and remain beloved by advanced readers as well.

  • Rider-Waite-Smith (RWS) — The most widely recognized tarot deck in the world. Its clear symbolism and fully illustrated minor arcana make it the gold standard for learning. Most tarot books and courses reference this deck, making it ideal for beginners building foundational knowledge.
  • Thoth Tarot (Aleister Crowley) — A complex, richly layered deck painted by Lady Frieda Harris. The Thoth deck integrates astrology, Kabbalah, and alchemical symbolism. Best suited for readers who enjoy deep esoteric study and want multi-dimensional interpretations.
  • Morgan-Greer Tarot — A borderless deck with vibrant, close-up imagery inspired by the RWS tradition. The bold colours and intimate compositions create an immediate emotional connection, making it wonderful for intuitive readers who respond to colour and feeling.

Modern and Contemporary Decks We Love

Modern tarot decks bring fresh perspectives, diverse representation, and contemporary art styles while honouring traditional meanings. These are some of our favourites for readers looking for something that feels current and personal.

  • The Wild Unknown — Created by Kim Krans, this deck features stunning nature-based illustrations with a minimalist colour palette. Animals, plants, and natural elements replace human figures, making it powerful for readers who connect with the natural world.
  • Modern Witch Tarot — Lisa Sterle reimagines the RWS system with diverse, contemporary characters in modern settings. This deck feels fresh and relatable while maintaining traditional tarot structure, making it perfect for readers who want representation and accessibility.
  • Moonchild Tarot — A dreamy, ethereal deck featuring mixed-media collage art. Each card feels like a portal into another dimension. Ideal for readers drawn to cosmic themes, astrology, and visionary art.

How to Choose the Right Tarot Deck for You

There is no single best tarot deck — only the best deck for you at this stage of your journey. Here are some factors to consider when making your choice.

  • Art style — Choose imagery that draws you in. If you cannot stop looking at the cards, you have found a match.
  • System — RWS-based decks are easiest to learn with because most resources follow this structure. Thoth and Marseille decks have their own systems.
  • Card size — Consider your hand size. Some decks are oversized and difficult to shuffle for smaller hands.
  • Guidebook quality — A thoughtful companion book can deepen your relationship with a new deck significantly.
  • Intuitive pull — Trust what draws you. Walk through our store on Lonsdale Avenue and notice which deck catches your eye first.

Frequently Asked Questions About Tarot Decks

Do I need to be gifted my first tarot deck?

This is a common myth with no basis in tarot tradition. You are absolutely welcome — and encouraged — to choose your own first deck. Selecting a deck yourself ensures it resonates with your personal energy and aesthetic preferences.

How many tarot decks should I own?

There is no right number. Some readers work exclusively with one deck for years, building a deep relationship with its imagery. Others collect multiple decks and choose based on the type of reading or their mood. Start with one deck you love, and let your collection grow naturally over time.

What is the difference between tarot and oracle decks?

Tarot decks follow a specific structure: 78 cards divided into Major Arcana (22 cards) and Minor Arcana (56 cards across four suits). Oracle decks have no fixed structure — they can contain any number of cards with any theme. Both are powerful tools; tarot offers more systematic depth, while oracle decks offer creative freedom.

Can I use tarot cards for meditation without doing readings?

Yes. Many people use tarot cards as visual meditation focuses, journal prompts, or creative inspiration. You can pull a single card each morning as a theme for reflection without interpreting it as a prediction. The imagery alone can spark powerful insights.
