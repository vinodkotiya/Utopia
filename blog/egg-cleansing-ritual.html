The Egg Cleansing Ritual: How to Clear Negative Energy With a Simple Egg

By Utopia Wellness & Gifts · August 22, 2026

An egg cleansing ritual — known as a limpia in Latin American traditions — is an ancient practice used to absorb and remove negative energy from your body and aura. By passing a raw egg over your body with focused intention, practitioners believe the egg draws out stagnant, heavy, or harmful energies that may be causing emotional, physical, or spiritual disturbance. It's one of the simplest yet most powerful cleansing tools available to anyone.

While the limpia is most associated with Mexican and Central American curanderismo (folk healing), egg cleansing rituals appear across many cultures. European folk traditions, particularly in Italy and Greece, have used eggs to diagnose and lift the evil eye (mal de ojo). West African and Filipino spiritual practices incorporate similar techniques. The universal thread is simple: the egg, as a vessel of life, has a unique capacity to absorb energetic imprints. How Does an Egg Cleansing Work?

The principle behind an egg cleansing is energetic absorption. Just as the egg contains the potential for life, it also has the capacity to take on and hold energy from its environment. When you pass a raw egg over your body — slowly, intentionally, and with prayer or focused thought — the egg is believed to absorb negativity from your auric field.

Think of it as an energetic sponge. Heavy emotions, other people's projections, psychic debris, and spiritual attachments can all accumulate in your energy body over time. The egg gently lifts these layers away, leaving you feeling lighter, clearer, and more grounded. After the cleansing, you crack the egg into water and read the patterns — this acts as a diagnostic tool, showing you what was cleared.

Step-by-Step Egg Cleansing Instructions

You can perform an egg cleansing on yourself at home. All you need is a fresh raw egg (white-shelled and room temperature works best), a clear glass of water, and a quiet space where you won't be interrupted.

1. Prepare Your Space

Cleanse your environment first. Light a candle, burn sage or palo santo, or simply open a window to invite fresh air. Fill a clear glass about three-quarters full with room-temperature water and set it nearby. Wash the egg under cool running water to remove any residual energy it may have picked up.

2. Set Your Intention

Hold the egg in both hands and close your eyes. State your intention — either silently or aloud. This might be: "I ask that this egg absorb all negative energy, heaviness, and spiritual blockages from my body and aura." Some people pray to their guides, ancestors, or higher power at this stage. The key is sincerity and focus.

3. Pass the Egg Over Your Body

Beginning at the crown of your head, slowly roll or hover the egg over your entire body in a downward motion. Move from head to face, neck, shoulders, arms, chest, stomach, hips, legs, and finally feet. Use gentle, sweeping motions. Pay extra attention to areas that feel heavy, tense, or blocked. Many practitioners move the egg in small circles over the heart, throat, and stomach areas. The process should take 5–10 minutes.

4. Crack the Egg Into Water

Once you've passed the egg over your entire body, gently crack it into the prepared glass of water. Be careful not to break the yolk. Set the glass down and let it rest for 5–10 minutes before reading it. This resting period allows the energy patterns to fully form.

How to Read the Egg After a Limpia

Reading the egg is both an art and an intuitive practice. Look at the glass from the side (not directly above) and observe the patterns that have formed in the water, whites, and yolk.

  • Bubbles — Small bubbles rising to the surface indicate that the egg successfully absorbed negative energy. The more bubbles, the more energy was cleared. Large bubbles may suggest that a significant amount of tension or heavy energy was released.
  • Cloudy or murky water — This suggests that stagnant or stuck energy was present in your field. It's been lifted, and the murkiness is evidence of what was released.
  • Strings or cobweb-like strands — White strands rising from the yolk to the surface often indicate energetic cords or attachments to other people, situations, or places that were draining you.
  • Blood spots or dark areas — These can indicate heavier energy that needed clearing — possibly from sustained stress, spiritual attack, or long-held emotional wounds. A second cleansing may be beneficial.
  • An eye shape — This is commonly interpreted as the evil eye (mal de ojo) being present and removed during the cleansing.
  • Clear water and a settled yolk — Congratulations. Your aura was relatively clean, and the egg confirms that your energy field is balanced and protected.

Aftercare: What to Do After Your Egg Cleansing

Proper disposal is important. Do not eat the egg or leave it sitting in your home. Flush the contents down the toilet or dispose of them at a crossroads (a traditional practice in some cultures). Rinse the glass with salt water. Wash your hands thoroughly.

After your cleansing, support your energy body with a salt bath. Add a cup of sea salt or Epsom salt to warm bath water and soak for 15–20 minutes. This seals the cleansed aura and helps you ground back into your body. You can also hold a piece of black tourmaline or selenite to strengthen your protective field.

Drink plenty of water, rest if you feel tired, and avoid crowded or energetically heavy environments for the remainder of the day. Some people feel emotional release, lightness, or gentle fatigue — all of these are normal responses to clearing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I do an egg cleansing?

Most practitioners recommend performing an egg cleansing when you feel energetically heavy, after stressful events, or once a month as general maintenance. If your reading shows heavy energy (blood spots, excessive cloudiness), you may want to repeat the ritual once a week for two to three weeks until the water runs clear.

Can I do an egg cleansing on someone else?

Yes. Egg cleansings can be performed on others, including children, pets, and even spaces. When cleansing another person, always ask for their consent and set your intention clearly. The process is the same — pass the egg over their body from head to feet while holding the intention to clear negative energy on their behalf.

Does the type of egg matter?

Traditionally, a fresh, raw, white-shelled egg at room temperature is preferred. Some practitioners believe that organic or farm-fresh eggs hold more vital energy and are therefore more effective at absorbing negativity. Avoid eggs that are cracked, old, or have been refrigerated for a long time — bring them to room temperature before use.

What if the egg breaks during the cleansing?

If the egg cracks or breaks before you've finished the ritual, this is traditionally interpreted as a sign that there was a significant amount of heavy energy to clear. Dispose of the broken egg carefully (flush it or discard it away from your home), cleanse your hands with salt water, and start fresh with a new egg.
