5 Spiritual Signs Someone Is Watching Over You From the Other Side

By Utopia Wellness & Gifts · May 18, 2026

When someone we love crosses over, the bond doesn't simply disappear. Many people who visit our store on Lonsdale Avenue in North Vancouver tell us the same thing: they feel their loved one's presence, but they're not sure if what they're experiencing is real. The truth is, spirit communicates in subtle, persistent ways — and once you learn to recognize the signs, you'll realize your loved ones have been reaching out all along.

These signs from the other side aren't random coincidences. They're intentional messages of comfort, love, and reassurance. Whether it's a feather appearing on your path or a song that plays at the perfect moment, spirit finds creative ways to let you know you're never truly alone. Below are five of the most common spiritual signs that someone is watching over you from heaven — and how to open yourself to receiving them.

1. Finding Feathers and Coins in Unexpected Places

One of the most widely reported signs from spirit is discovering feathers or coins in places where they shouldn't logically be. You might find a white feather on your kitchen counter, inside a closed book, or resting on the driver's seat of your car. Pennies and dimes appear on sidewalks at the exact moment you're thinking of your loved one.

These small tokens are spirit's way of saying, "I'm here. I see you." The key is the timing and context. A feather found on a windy day outdoors might be ordinary — but one that appears inside your home, or shows up repeatedly during moments of grief or decision-making, carries a deeper significance. Many spiritual traditions view white feathers as angelic calling cards, while coins — especially dimes — are associated with messages from loved ones who have crossed over.

What to do: When you find a feather or coin, pause and acknowledge it. Say thank you — out loud or silently. Notice what you were thinking about at that exact moment. Keep a small journal of these encounters; over time, you may notice patterns that confirm the connection.

2. Electrical Disturbances and Flickering Lights

Spirit exists as energy, and one of the easiest ways for those on the other side to make their presence known is through electrical interference. Lights flickering without explanation, a television turning on by itself, your phone lighting up with no notification, or a song suddenly playing on a device you didn't touch — these are all common signs of spirit communication.

Many of our clients in North Vancouver describe moments where a specific light in their home flickers every time they mention their loved one's name, or where a digital clock repeatedly shows a number that was meaningful to the person who passed. This isn't your wiring — it's energy reaching across the veil.

What to do: Rather than feeling frightened, lean into curiosity. When lights flicker or electronics behave strangely, speak aloud: "I know you're here. Thank you for showing me." Pay attention to what happens next — often the disturbance will intensify briefly as confirmation, then settle.

3. Vivid Dreams and Visitation Experiences

Dreams are one of the most powerful channels for spirit communication because our conscious mind is at rest, and our defences are lowered. A visitation dream feels fundamentally different from a regular dream — it's vivid, coherent, and emotionally intense. You wake up with a deep sense of peace or with a clear message. The colours are more saturated, the conversation feels real, and you remember every detail even days or weeks later.

In these dreams, your loved one often appears healthy, radiant, and at peace. They may deliver a simple message — "I'm okay," "I love you," or guidance about a decision you're facing. Sometimes they appear just to embrace you, offering the comfort your waking mind has been craving.

  • Visitation dreams feel more real than regular dreams — you can recall specific details upon waking
  • Your loved one appears whole and at peace — often younger or healthier than when they passed
  • There is a clear message or feeling — comfort, forgiveness, guidance, or simply love
  • You wake with a sense of calm — rather than the confusion or fear of a nightmare

What to do: Before sleep, set the intention to receive a visit. Place a photo of your loved one on your nightstand or hold a piece of amethyst — known for enhancing spiritual dreams. Keep a journal beside your bed and write down everything immediately upon waking, before the details fade.

4. Familiar Scents With No Physical Source

Scent is deeply tied to memory, and spirit knows this. Many people report suddenly smelling their grandmother's perfume, their father's pipe tobacco, or a mother's signature cooking — with no possible physical source nearby. These phantom scents are called "clairalience" in mediumship, and they're one of the most emotionally stirring signs that a loved one is near.

The scent typically arrives suddenly and fades quickly. It might happen when you're alone in a room, driving your car, or walking through a place that has no connection to the smell. Others around you may not detect it at all — because the message is meant specifically for you.

What to do: When a familiar scent washes over you, close your eyes and breathe it in. Acknowledge the presence with gratitude. These moments are gifts — brief but profoundly healing. If you'd like to deepen the connection, a mediumship session can help you understand why your loved one is reaching out at that particular time.

5. Meaningful Songs, Animals, and Perfect Timing

Have you ever turned on the radio and heard "your song" at the exact moment you needed comfort? Or noticed a cardinal, butterfly, or dragonfly appear repeatedly — especially during significant moments like anniversaries, birthdays, or times of difficult decisions? Spirit often works through synchronicity, orchestrating meaningful moments in the physical world to catch your attention.

Cardinals are widely associated with departed loved ones — the bright red flash of colour is hard to miss, which is exactly the point. Butterflies represent transformation and the continuity of the soul. Hawks and eagles suggest your loved one is watching from a higher perspective. And songs — particularly ones that play at impossible times or in unlikely places — are spirit's way of reminding you that love transcends the physical world.

What to do: Start paying attention to patterns. If a specific bird, song, or number keeps appearing, take note of when and where it shows up. Ask your loved one for a specific sign — "Send me a butterfly if you're with me" — and then watch. Spirit responds to direct requests more often than you might expect.

How to Strengthen Your Connection to the Other Side

Recognizing these signs is the first step. Deepening the connection takes intention and openness. Here are a few grounded practices that can help you become more receptive to messages from spirit:

  • Meditation — Quieting the mind creates space for subtle impressions to come through
  • Journaling — Recording signs helps you identify patterns and validate your experiences
  • Working with crystals — Amethyst, celestite, and angelite are known for enhancing spiritual communication
  • Mediumship sessions — A skilled medium can bridge the gap and deliver specific, evidential messages from your loved ones
  • Creating a sacred space — A small altar with photos, candles, and meaningful objects invites connection

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if a sign is from a loved one or just a coincidence?

The key difference is timing and repetition. A sign from spirit typically arrives at a meaningful moment — when you're thinking of your loved one, making a decision they'd care about, or experiencing grief. If the same sign appears repeatedly (always finding dimes, always seeing cardinals at pivotal moments), trust that it's more than chance. Your intuition will also confirm it — you'll feel a warmth, a knowing, or an emotional response that ordinary coincidence doesn't produce.

Can anyone receive signs from heaven, or do you need to be psychic?

You don't need to be psychic to receive signs from a departed loved one. Spirit reaches out to everyone — the difference is simply awareness and openness. The more you pay attention and acknowledge signs when they appear, the more frequently you'll notice them. Think of it as a conversation: the more you respond, the more spirit communicates.

What if I'm not receiving any signs at all?

Grief can sometimes create an energetic heaviness that makes it harder to perceive subtle signs. This doesn't mean your loved one isn't trying — it simply means the channel needs a bit of clearing. Practices like meditation, spending time in nature, and working with a medium can help open that connection. Many of our clients at Utopia find that after their first mediumship session, they begin noticing signs they had previously overlooked.

What crystals can help me connect with loved ones in spirit?

Amethyst enhances spiritual awareness and is excellent for dream communication. Celestite is known as the stone of angelic connection and can open channels to higher realms. Angelite supports peaceful communication with the spirit world. You can hold these during meditation, place them under your pillow for visitation dreams, or keep them on a small altar dedicated to your loved one.
