How to Make Moon Water: A Simple Ritual for Lunar Energy

By Utopia Wellness & Gifts · October 22, 2026

To make moon water, fill a clean glass jar with filtered water, set an intention, and place it under the moonlight overnight — ideally during a full moon for maximum potency. Moon water is water that has been energetically charged by the moon's light, infusing it with lunar energy that can be used for cleansing crystals, enhancing rituals, watering plants, adding to baths, and even drinking as part of your spiritual practice.

Making moon water is one of the simplest yet most powerful rituals you can incorporate into your spiritual practice. It requires nothing more than water, a glass container, and moonlight — yet it creates a versatile sacred tool you can use throughout the lunar cycle. Cultures around the world have honoured water's ability to absorb and carry energy, and the moon's influence on water is something we can observe in ocean tides. In this guide, we'll walk you through the process step by step, explain the difference between full moon and new moon water, and share all the ways you can use this lunar-charged water in your daily life.

How to Make Moon Water Step by Step

  • Choose your container — Use a clean glass jar, bottle, or bowl. Glass is preferred because it doesn't interfere with energy transfer. Avoid plastic, which can leach chemicals and block subtle energy.
  • Fill with water — Use filtered, spring, or distilled water. If you plan to drink it, ensure your water source is safe for consumption.
  • Set your intention — Hold the container and clearly state or think your intention. For example: "I charge this water with the full moon's energy of release and clarity." Your intention shapes what the water carries.
  • Place under moonlight — Set your container outside or on a windowsill where moonlight can reach it. It doesn't need to be in direct moonbeams — even cloudy nights carry lunar energy. Leave it overnight from dusk to dawn.
  • Optional: Add crystals around (not in) the water — Place moonstone, clear quartz, or amethyst around your container to amplify the charging. Only place water-safe crystals inside if you choose to do so (never malachite, selenite, or pyrite in water).
  • Retrieve before extended sun exposure — Collect your moon water in the morning before the sun warms it significantly, as sunlight carries different energy and can override the lunar charge.

Full Moon Water vs. New Moon Water

The moon phase you choose determines the energy your water carries:

  • Full Moon Water — Carries energy of illumination, completion, release, gratitude, and heightened intuition. Best for: cleansing rituals, releasing old patterns, charging divination tools, amplifying psychic ability, and celebrating what you've accomplished.
  • New Moon Water — Carries energy of new beginnings, planting seeds, setting intentions, quiet growth, and going inward. Best for: manifestation rituals, intention-setting ceremonies, starting new projects, and nurturing new relationships.
  • Waxing Moon Water — Carries energy of growth and building. Good for attracting, expanding, and drawing things toward you.
  • Waning Moon Water — Carries energy of decreasing and letting go. Good for banishing, removing obstacles, and releasing habits.

Ways to Use Your Moon Water

  • Drink it — Sip moon water as part of your morning ritual or add it to tea. Set an intention with each sip. (Only drink if made with safe, filtered water in a clean container.)
  • Add to your bath — Pour moon water into your bath for a lunar-charged self-care ritual. Full moon water baths are especially powerful for emotional release.
  • Cleanse crystals — Use moon water to cleanse water-safe crystals. It combines the purifying power of water with lunar energy.
  • Water your plants — Plants respond beautifully to moon water. Use it for your houseplants or garden, especially when planting new seeds during the new moon.
  • Anoint yourself or objects — Dab moon water on your pulse points, third eye, or heart centre. Use it to bless candles, tarot decks, or ritual tools.
  • Add to cleaning water — Add moon water to your floor wash or spray bottle for an energetic cleanse of your living space.
  • Use in spell work and rituals — Moon water is a foundational ingredient in many spiritual practices and can enhance any ritual work aligned with the moon phase used.

Storage and Safety Notes

  • Store in glass — Keep moon water in sealed glass containers away from direct sunlight to preserve the lunar charge
  • Refrigerate if drinking — Moon water intended for consumption should be refrigerated and used within 1-2 weeks
  • Label your jars — Note the date, moon phase, and intention on each batch so you know which energy it carries
  • Never use toxic crystals in drinking water — If you added crystals, ensure they're 100% water-safe. When in doubt, place crystals around the jar, not inside it
  • Trust your senses — If moon water looks or smells off, use it for plants or cleaning rather than consuming it

Frequently Asked Questions

Does moon water work if it's cloudy?

Yes. The moon's gravitational and energetic influence reaches your water regardless of cloud cover — just as the moon still affects ocean tides on cloudy nights. Clouds don't block lunar energy. While clear-sky moonlight creates the strongest visual ritual experience, your water is still being charged energetically even through clouds.

Can I make moon water on a windowsill?

Absolutely. A windowsill that faces the moon works perfectly — the glass of your window doesn't block the energetic charge. This is especially practical in apartments, during winter months, or in areas where outdoor placement isn't safe. The intention you set matters more than the exact placement.

How long does moon water stay charged?

Moon water retains its energetic charge for approximately one lunar cycle (about 28 days) when stored properly in sealed glass away from sunlight. Many practitioners make fresh moon water each full moon and use their supply throughout the month. If stored well, it can maintain potency even longer, though freshly made water carries the strongest energy.

Is it safe to drink moon water?

Yes, provided you used clean filtered or spring water and a food-safe glass container. Do not drink moon water if crystals were placed directly in the water (unless you're certain they're non-toxic and water-safe), if the container was left uncovered where debris could enter, or if the water has been stored for an extended period without refrigeration.
