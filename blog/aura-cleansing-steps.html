How to Cleanse Your Aura: Simple Steps for Energetic Refreshment

September 25, 2026

Your aura picks up energetic debris from every interaction, environment, and emotional experience throughout the day. If you feel heavy, foggy, or drained after crowds, difficult conversations, or charged environments, your energy field has accumulated material that does not belong to you. Cleansing it is as practical as washing your hands after contact with something unclean.

Aura cleansing does not require special gifts or expensive equipment. Water, salt, breath, sound, and focused intention all work effectively. The key is conscious engagement: performing the action while holding the clear intention that foreign energy releases and your field restores to its natural frequency.

Six Steps for Aura Cleansing

Step 1: Ground yourself. Stand with bare feet on the earth or press them firmly on the floor. Imagine roots extending from your feet into the ground. This creates a stable foundation before you begin releasing anything.

Step 2: Set intention. State clearly (aloud or internally): "I release all energy that is not mine. I return to my own frequency." Intention directs the process. Without it, physical actions alone carry less effect.

Step 3: Use water. Shower with the intention that water carries away accumulated energy. Visualize grey or murky light washing off your body and flowing down the drain. If a full shower is unavailable, washing hands and face with this intention works as a quick alternative.

Step 4: Salt. Add a handful of sea salt or Epsom salt to bathwater and soak for 15 to 20 minutes. Salt draws out absorbed energy through the skin. If bathing is impractical, rubbing salt between wet palms and rinsing works for a targeted hand-cleanse.

Step 5: Brush your aura. Using your hands (or a selenite wand), sweep downward along your body about 3 to 6 inches from the skin. Start at the crown of your head and brush down to your feet, as though brushing invisible dust off your energy field. Shake your hands after each sweep to release what they collected.

Step 6: Seal your field. Visualize a bubble of golden or white light surrounding your body, about arm's length in every direction. Intend that this boundary allows love and connection through while filtering out energy that does not serve you. This protective step prevents immediate re-accumulation.

When to Cleanse

After time in crowds or busy public spaces. Following difficult conversations or conflict. When returning home from work where you interact with many people. After energy healing sessions (both giving and receiving). Whenever you feel inexplicably heavy, irritable, or drained without a clear personal cause. Before sleep to prevent carrying the day's residue into your dreams.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I cleanse my aura?

Daily if you are highly sensitive or work in environments with many people. At minimum, anytime you notice unexplained heaviness or mood changes that do not match your actual circumstances.

Can other people's energy actually affect me?

Yes, particularly for empathic or energetically sensitive people. Others' emotions, stress, and mental states leave residue in shared spaces that sensitive people absorb without conscious awareness.

How do I know if heaviness is mine or absorbed?

Ask yourself: "Did I feel this way before I entered that space or interacted with that person?" If the heaviness appeared after contact with a specific environment or person, it is likely absorbed rather than generated by your own circumstances.
