Long "About / Media" (220–280 words)

Utopia Wellness & Gifts is a North Vancouver store dedicated to premium gifts, healing crystals, and personal clarity. The shop began in the 1990s, founded by Donna King near Lonsdale Quay Market. It later moved along Lonsdale Avenue — including a location near 3rd Street — before arriving at its current home at 1826 Lonsdale Ave. Over the years the store has had several owners. Wendy took over in 2021 (then known as Utopia Sacred Space). Current owners Vinod and Olga renovated the space and repositioned it as Utopia Wellness & Gifts. Today Utopia serves people looking for meaningful gifts, wellness tools, and in-person guidance. The store carries healing crystals, jewelry, incense, candles, home décor, and locally made pieces. Services include Clarity & Guidance sessions (tarot, intuitive readings, mediumship, and related practices), workshops, New Moon circles, and wellness experiences such as Mind Spa and Dream Machine sessions. Utopia is designed for everyday customers as well as those seeking spiritual tools — from first-time visitors to regulars who return for readings and restock consumables. Visit: 1826 Lonsdale Ave, North Vancouver Phone: 604-984-8782 Online: UtopiaStore.ca Parking: Free parking at the back of the store Hours: See UtopiaStore.ca or Google for current hours
