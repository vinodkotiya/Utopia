Micro Guidance & Clarity

The Mini Guide

Short, plain-language answers to the small questions people bring us — crystals, palmistry, signs and symbols, tarot, astrology, and rituals. A quick reference from our team at Utopia Wellness & Gifts on Lonsdale Ave, North Vancouver.

Crystals Palmistry Signs & Symbols Tarot Astrology Rituals

Crystals

Metaphysical meaning of amethyst

Amethyst, plainly: a purple quartz many people keep for calm and clear-headedness. In everyday crystal practice it is reached for during stressful stretches or before sleep, more as a steadying companion than a fix. Some people rest one on the nightstand and simply hold it on a hard evening.

How to cleanse crystals with moonlight

To cleanse crystals with moonlight, most people set the stones on a windowsill overnight, ideally around a full moon, and bring them back in by morning. A common mistake is leaving water-sensitive stones out in the damp, so keep them dry and simply let the light do the work.

Palmistry

What does a broken life line mean

A broken life line is commonly read as a period of significant change rather than anything to fear. Readers usually look at both hands together and at the surrounding lines before drawing any conclusion, since a single mark rarely tells the whole story.

Signs & Symbols

Metaphysical meaning of a dragonfly

In folk symbolism, a dragonfly is usually taken as a gentle sign of change and seeing a situation more clearly. What it points to depends on what was already on your mind when it appeared, so treat it as a prompt to pause rather than a fixed message.

Tarot

What does the tower card mean

What does the Tower card usually mean? Most readers take it as sudden, clearing change that makes room for something more honest. The card is read in the context of the cards around it, so it is rarely as alarming as it first looks.

Astrology

What is a rising sign

Your rising sign, plainly: the zodiac sign that was on the eastern horizon at your birth, often described as how you meet the world. It colours first impressions and your instinctive style, which is why two people with the same sun sign can come across quite differently.

Rituals

How to set an intention for the new moon

To set a new moon intention, most people write one clear, present-tense line and keep it somewhere they will see it. A common mistake is listing ten things at once, so choosing a single focus tends to work better.
