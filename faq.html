What is the difference between white sage and palo santo?

White sage delivers a strong, thorough cleanse — ideal for a full energetic reset after conflict or a big life change. Palo santo is lighter and sweeter, better suited to everyday use when you want to invite good energy in rather than clear a heavy atmosphere out. Many customers keep both on hand for different moments.
