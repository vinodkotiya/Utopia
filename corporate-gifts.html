Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a good corporate gift?

A good corporate gift is thoughtful, useful, and aligned with wellness. At Utopia, we focus on crystals, calming tools, and experiences that employees and clients actually use and appreciate.

Do you offer bulk pricing for corporate orders?

Yes. We offer competitive bulk pricing on curated gift boxes and custom crystal sets. Email us with your quantity and budget for a custom proposal.

Can you deliver corporate gifts across Canada?

Yes. We ship throughout Canada and also offer local delivery and pickup in North Vancouver.

Do you offer private wellness experiences for teams?

Yes. We host private Dream Machine sound & light sessions and wellness workshops for corporate teams in North Vancouver.
