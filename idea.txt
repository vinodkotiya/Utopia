About Us – Utopia Store
North Vancouver’s Metaphysical Sanctuary Since 1997 ✨
For over 30 years, Utopia Store has been more than just a shop — we’ve been a sacred space where hearts heal, spirits awaken, and magic becomes real.
We’re a family-owned sanctuary in North Vancouver dedicated to harmony, happiness, and deep healing. Here, you’ll find more than beautiful products — you’ll find genuine connection, compassionate guidance, and tools that support you through life’s biggest challenges: illness, loss, transition, or simply the weight of everyday stress.
Every crystal, sage bundle, tarot deck, and handcrafted treasure we carry is chosen with intention. Many are locally made by Canadian artisans, while others are unique, one-of-a-kind pieces sourced with love from around the world. Whether you’re shopping in-store in North Vancouver or from anywhere in Canada through our online store, you’ll experience the difference of touching the energy, speaking with real people who care, and taking home something truly special.
